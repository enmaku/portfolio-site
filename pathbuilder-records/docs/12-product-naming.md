# 12 — Product Naming

Naming research for the mobile-first Pathfinder 2e character builder. This document
records the constraints, the method, and the verified results so the decision is
reproducible and the reasoning survives.

**Status:** candidate list verified against the `.com` registry on 2026-11-18.

**Decision (2026-11-18):** the product is named **Critcrafter** — `critcrafter.com`.
Chosen from the "crafter" family (§3.4) for its brevity, pronounceability, and the
"crit" root (a core PF2e term).

---

## 1. Constraints

The name must satisfy three independent constraints, each from a different rights
holder.

### 1.1 Trademark — Pathbuilder (Redrazors)

From [11-legal-and-licensing.md](./11-legal-and-licensing.md) §5:

> A name incorporating "Pathbuilder" (or a confusingly similar variant) for a
> Pathfinder-character-builder product creates a **meaningful** risk of confusion.
> **Choose a distinct name.** Do not use "Pathbuilder," "Path Builder," or close variants.

So the name must **not** contain "pathbuilder", "path builder", or any close variant
("pathbuild", "pathforge" is fine — "forge" is generic — but "pathbuilder" itself is
off-limits).

### 1.2 Product Identity — Paizo

From [11-legal-and-licensing.md](./11-legal-and-licensing.md) §9:

> **Product Identity is off-limits**: "Pathfinder," the Pathfinder logo, Golarion
> setting names, iconic character names, and Paizo's trade dress require a separate
> license.

So the name must **not** contain "Pathfinder", "Golarion", or any Paizo setting or
iconic-character name. It may *refer* to Pathfinder nominatively in marketing copy
("a Pathfinder 2e character builder") with a non-affiliation disclaimer, but the
product name itself must be clean.

### 1.3 Domain availability

The user wants a `.com` address. Every candidate below was checked against the
authoritative registry (Verisign RDAP, `rdap.verisign.com`), not a registrar's
availability API. `404` = unregistered (available); `200` = registered (taken).

---

## 2. Method

1. **Vocabulary extraction.** Mined the corpus for domain keywords: rules terms
   (feat, spell, ancestry, heritage, proficiency, crit, hero point, focus, fortune,
   legendary), the builder's own nouns (sheet, tome, codex, grimoire, compendium,
   forge, foundry, workshop), and evocative fantasy nouns (sigil, rune, glyph, relic,
   shard, hoard, cache, satchel, caravan, lodge, anvil, crucible, odyssey, saga,
   legend, myth, fable).
2. **Combinatorial generation.** Produced ~18,900 two-word compounds and ~28,800
   coined/portmanteau forms from those roots.
3. **Registry check.** Queried Verisign RDAP for every candidate's `.com` (concurrency
   6, ~47,700 total lookups). Result: ~9,100 compounds and ~21,200 coined forms are
   unregistered.
4. **Curation.** Filtered to pronounceable, brandable names of 5–14 characters with no
   awkward letter runs, then hand-picked the strongest patterns.

**Finding:** every single dictionary word (hero, legend, forge, tome, codex, …) is
already registered as a `.com`. The available space is entirely in **well-formed
two-word compounds** — which is also the most brandable shape for a product name.

## 4. Top picks (author's shortlist)

The strongest candidates, balancing memorability, pronounceability, and the
"character sheet / character builder" meaning:

1. **featsheet.com** — "feat" is *the* Pathfinder 2e term (5,172 feats in the data),
   and "sheet" names the artifact. Short, obvious, on-the-nose.
2. **featbuilder.com** — same root, names the function directly.
3. **sigilsheet.com** / **sigiltome.com** — "sigil" is evocative, fantasy-adjacent,
   and not tied to any rights holder.
4. **grimoiretome.com** / **grimoiresheet.com** — "grimoire" is the spellbook; strong
   for a tool that manages spellcasting (1,379 spells).
5. **rollcallforge.com** — "roll call" puns on dice-rolling and the party roster;
   "forge" on building. Distinctive and memorable.
6. **satchelforge.com** / **satchelsheet.com** — "satchel" evokes the adventurer's
   kit, mobile-friendly connotation.
7. **caravanforge.com** — "caravan" evokes the party on the road; fits a mobile tool.
8. **anviltome.com** / **crucibletome.com** — crafting imagery, strong and short.

---

## 5. Notes and caveats

- **Availability is a snapshot.** `.com` status was checked 2026-11-18 against Verisign
  RDAP. A name can be registered at any moment; re-check before committing, and
  register promptly once chosen.
- **Trademark clearance is separate from domain availability.** An available `.com`
  does not mean the name is free of third-party trademark claims. Before launch, run a
  USPTO TESS search and (per [11-legal-and-licensing.md](./11-legal-and-licensing.md)
  §10) consult an IP attorney on the final name.
- **Avoid the taken "forge" names.** `critforge`, `featforge`, `spellforge`,
  `tomeforge`, `codexforge`, `grimoireforge`, `sigilforge`, `runeforge`, `glyphforge`,
  `relicforge`, `shardforge`, `hoardforge`, `cacheforge`, `questforge`, `sagaforge`,
  `legendforge`, `mythforge`, `epicforge`, `fableforge`, `odysseyforge`,
  `journeyforge`, `ventureforge`, `anvilforge`, `crucibleforge`, `foundryforge`,
  `scrollforge`, `diceforge`, `rollforge`, and `heroforge` are **all registered** —
  the "forge" suffix is heavily mined, so the available forge names above are the
  remaining good ones.
- **The full raw results** (all ~9,100 available compounds and ~21,200 available coined
  forms) are not reproduced here; they were generated and checked programmatically and
  can be regenerated with the same script if a different name is wanted.
